Deepfake Audio
Detection

Identify whether an audio clip is genuine or manipulated using advanced signal analysis techniques.

With the rapid growth of synthetic media, deepfake audio has become a major concern. This system analyzes speech patterns, frequency variations, and acoustic features such as MFCC and LFCC to accurately determine whether an audio file is real or fake.

It helps in preventing misinformation, fraud, and voice cloning attacks by providing a reliable detection mechanism for audio authenticity.
